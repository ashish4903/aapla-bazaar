# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- My shop: a vendor writes the shop name, place, WhatsApp number and an optional UPI ID, picks items from a list of common produce (or types a new one), and sets each price, unit and whether it is in stock. It is saved on the vendor's phone.
- Send today's rates on WhatsApp: one tap builds a message with the rate list and a link to the shop. The link can also be copied or previewed.
- Shop page from the vendor's link: today's items and prices (per kilo, bunch, piece or dozen), sold-out items marked, search, filters by kind, and a warning when the rates are from an earlier day.
- Basket: loose produce from a quarter kilo, everything else one by one. It is kept on the phone, per shop, if the page is closed.
- Order on WhatsApp: name, pickup or home delivery with an address, and a note become a ready-typed message to the shop's number. Quantities can still be changed on the order screen. Name and address are remembered on the phone only.
- After ordering: a "Pay by UPI" button with the amount filled in (when the shop has a UPI ID), and "fill my last order again" on the next visit.
- Home page that offers the shop opened last and explains how a shopkeeper starts. Share button for a shop. Marathi and English throughout.

### Changed

- A fresher look: softer cards, a picked item is highlighted, small animations on adding and on the basket, larger tap areas, and a dark theme.

### Removed

- The demo shop and its invented prices. The app now shows only what a vendor has typed.
