# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- My shop, set up once in three short steps: shop name and WhatsApp number, what the shop sells (from a list of common produce, or a typed name), and how customers are served (pickup, home delivery with an optional charge and minimum order, timings, UPI ID). It is saved on the seller's phone.
- The seller's daily screen: open or closed today, a note for customers, and each item with large price down/up buttons, the last price sent, a sold-out switch and a star for today's specials. Items and shop details sit behind a bottom bar.
- Send today's rates on WhatsApp: one tap builds a message with the note, the rate list and a link to the shop. The screen then says when the rates were sent, and asks for a resend if they change. The link can also be copied or previewed.
- Shop page from the seller's link. The top answers a buyer's first questions: open or closed with timings, the seller's note, pickup or delivery, delivery charge and minimum order, and a call button. Today's specials come first, then items by kind (per kilo, bunch, piece or dozen), with search, filters, sold-out items marked, and a warning when the rates are from an earlier day. A closed shop shows its rates but takes no orders.
- Basket: loose produce jumps to 250 g, 500 g, 1 kg or 2 kg in one tap, or steps by a quarter kilo; everything else one by one. The bar shows the total and how much more is needed for home delivery. It is kept on the phone, per shop.
- Order as a bill: items, delivery charge and total, with quantities still changeable. Name, address and pickup-or-delivery are remembered on the phone only, and the order becomes a ready-typed WhatsApp message to the shop's number.
- After ordering: what happens next in three steps, a "Pay by UPI" button with the amount filled in (when the shop has a UPI ID), and "fill my last order again" on the next visit.
- Home page that offers the shop opened last and explains how a shopkeeper starts. A seller who opens the app lands on their own day. Share button for a shop. Marathi and English throughout.
- Add to phone: where the browser allows it, a button puts the app on the home screen with its own icon. Once opened, the app also loads on a weak or missing network.

### Changed

- Items are picture tiles, two to a row, with less to read.
- The order slides up over the shop instead of replacing it, and closes with ✕, a tap outside or Escape.
- A returning customer's name and address show as one line with a Change button, and "fill my last order again" opens the order ready to send.
- A short buzz answers each tap on phones that have one. A seller's price is selected when tapped, so typing replaces it.
- A fresher look: softer cards, a picked item is highlighted, small animations on adding and on the basket, larger tap areas, and a dark theme.

### Removed

- The demo shop and its invented prices. The app now shows only what a vendor has typed.
