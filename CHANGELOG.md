### Added

- Shop page with today's items and prices (per kilo, bunch or piece), sold-out items marked, in Marathi and English.
- Basket: loose vegetables from a quarter kilo, everything else one by one. It is kept on the phone if the page is closed.
- Order on WhatsApp: name, pickup or home delivery with an address, and a note become a ready-typed message to the shop. Name and address are remembered on the phone only.
- A demo shop (prices are not real) until the vendor screen and database arrive.
